Answers

  1. A <div> is block: it takes the full available width, starts on a new line, and respects width, height, margin and padding.
  2. A <span> is inline: it only takes the width of its content, stays on the same line, and ignores width/height and vertical margin.
  3. inline-block sits on the same line like inline, but accepts width, height, margin and padding like block.
  4. display: flex makes the element a flex container: its direct children become flex items laid out along a main axis (a row by default), side by side.

Padding vs margin

  1. padding changes the space between the content and the border.
  2. margin changes the space between the element's border and its neighbours.
  3. Padding creates space inside the element.
  4. Margin creates space outside the element.
